Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

ClinicBook Pro ("we", "our", "us") provides a clinic management and appointment booking platform used by clinics and their patients. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website, booking pages, and WhatsApp messaging services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name, phone number, and appointment details you provide when booking
  • Messages you send to us via WhatsApp when interacting with a clinic's AI receptionist or staff
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and IP address for security and performance purposes

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Schedule, confirm, and manage appointments
  • Respond to inquiries via WhatsApp, including through automated (AI-assisted) replies
  • Improve our services and troubleshoot technical issues

WhatsApp Messaging

If you message a clinic through WhatsApp, your messages may be processed by our platform, including by an AI assistant, to help schedule appointments or answer questions. A human staff member can take over the conversation at any time.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the specific clinic you are booking with, and with service providers (such as our hosting and messaging infrastructure providers) strictly to operate the platform.

Data Retention

We retain appointment and messaging data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 4, 2026

If you would like your personal data deleted from ClinicBook Pro, including appointment history and WhatsApp message records, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to [bushmaina857@gmail.com] with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", including:

  • Your full name
  • The phone number used to book appointments or message us on WhatsApp
  • The name of the clinic you interacted with (if known)

What Happens Next

We will verify your request and delete your personal data from our systems within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain records for legal, medical, or regulatory compliance purposes.

Contact
